1. This is a customer-facing dashboard where organization members manage
   shared AI support conversations and document metadata in their workspace.

2. Documents are associated with a conversation [clarified].

3. Title of the conversation is entered manually for now.

4. Messages are stored under a conversation with roles and timestamps.

5. Use only substring matching for searching.

New requirement - Organizations

6. All members in an organization have equal permissions for now.

7. Metadata and messages are interlaced chronologically in a unified
   conversation timeline.
   - Metadata events are displayed at their recorded timestamp alongside
     messages, rather than in a separate activity feed.
   - Metadata entries are visually distinct from user and assistant messages
     so they provide context without being mistaken for chat content.
   - A single timeline preserves the sequence of conversation activity and
     lets users understand what changed in the context in which it happened.
